09 / BACKGROUND

ABOUT ELECT

The narrative behind the multidisciplinary approach.

PORT HARCOURT / NIGERIA

Combining technical rigor with visual creativity to solve complex problems from both sides of the spectrum.

I've never been very good at staying in one box. My curiosity about technology and my refusal to settle for average have always pushed me toward finding better ways to solve problems. What started as an inborn interest evolved into a multidisciplinary journey through video editing, live production, hardware engineering, web development, graphic design and cybersecurity. Today, I work at the intersection of creativity, technology and storytelling. I've edited content published across the internet that has collectively generated 5M+ views, created 100+ graphic design projects, developed digital experiences, worked as a videographer and built practical experience in computer hardware and cybersecurity. I've also worked with brands including Syndicate Wears, The Macedonia Call and Favours Estate.

But the work isn't just about the skills. Creativity is my calling. I believe creativity is God's given solution to man's constant inefficiency in trying to make the world a better place. Whether I'm editing a film, designing a brand, building a website or solving a technical problem, my goal is the same: Make it better. Make it matter. Make people say wow.

BUILDING WITH PEOPLE My journey has also been shaped by leadership. I've served as Director of Software at NACOS UNIPORT, where I contributed to technology initiatives and student development; organized the NACOS Creators Summit; led a 10-day Tech Digest campaign; and spoke to 100-level Computing students about building intentional careers in technology. As a Content Manager at Student Shop Nigeria, I created 250+ pieces of content, helping expand the brand's visibility and convert attention into returning customers. At WCCCF UniPort, I've worked across graphic design, video editing and livestream management as a Media Operator. I also founded DECRYPT, a movement built around young technology enthusiasts, with its flagship annual conference beginning with DECRYPT 1.0 - The Code Behind Creativity. And I'm still building. My ambition stretches from creating world-class digital experiences to eventually telling stories on the scale of Hollywood blockbusters. The disciplines may keep changing. The mission doesn't: Keep learning. Keep creating. Keep building. Never settle for average.
